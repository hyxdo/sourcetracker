# Marlow Bay Adopted Budget, Fiscal 2024

## Harbor and shoreline

The council allocated $2.4 million to harbor dredging for fiscal 2024.

The parks department received $800,000 for shoreline trail repairs.

No funds were designated for reef or kelp restoration.
