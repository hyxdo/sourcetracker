# Options for Restoring the Northern Reef

## Background

Urchin density on the northern reef nearly tripled between 2019 and 2022 [@tribune-report].

The kelp canopy above the northern reef shrank by sixty percent [@tribune-report].

Reefs near the otter raft held forty percent fewer urchins than reefs farther away [@otter-study].

## Conclusion

The northern reef needs urchin removal before its kelp canopy disappears.

Protecting the otter raft would keep urchins scarce on nearby reefs.

Fewer urchins on the northern reef, whether from otters or from removal, would let the kelp canopy recover.
