# Foraging by Tagged Sea Otters in Marlow Bay

## Results

Tagged sea otters ate an average of fourteen urchins per hour on active feeding dives.

Reefs within two kilometers of the otter raft held forty percent fewer urchins than comparable reefs farther away.

The otter raft grew from nine animals to twenty-six between 2018 and 2022.
