# Marlow Bay Reef Survey, Final Report

## Urchin density

Divers counted purple urchins on forty fixed transects between 2019 and 2022. Urchin density on the northern reef rose from four to eleven animals per square meter over the survey period.

Kelp canopy over the northern reef shrank by sixty percent during the same four years.

## Water conditions

Sea surface temperature at the harbor mouth averaged 1.2 degrees above the twenty-year mean.

Nutrient levels showed no consistent trend across the sampling stations.
