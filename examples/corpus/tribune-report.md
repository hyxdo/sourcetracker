# Urchins Are Eating Marlow Bay's Kelp

Urchin density on the northern reef nearly tripled between 2019 and 2022, according to [a survey by the marine institute](https://marlowbay.example/reports/reef-survey).

The kelp canopy above the northern reef shrank by sixty percent over those four years [@harbor-survey].

Reefs close to the otter raft held forty percent fewer urchins than reefs farther away, [researchers reported](otter-study).

Local divers say the change is visible on nearly every trip.
